04

Method and data compatibility

This static entry module is designed for the event-level ZeroWaste workflow used in institutional catering research.

Physical measurements

Only returned plate waste is measured, in kilograms. Serving temperatures are recorded in degrees Celsius. Enter soups and main courses separately.

Serving quantities

Soup quantities are standardised to litres (L), while main-course quantities are standardised to kilograms (kg). Decilitres, decagrams and grams are converted arithmetically. Portion and piece counts require a user-supplied, verified conversion factor; otherwise the standardised quantity is left blank.

Sensory scale

Staff rate appearance, odour, taste, texture and overall liking from 1 (very poor) to 5 (very good). These are staff ratings, not individual child questionnaires.

Record linkage

The export preserves each event identifier, institution, date, age group, dish names and measurements for integration with separate consumer and menu records in the ZeroWaste R pipeline.

Scope: This GitHub Pages site is a local-first data-entry client, not the R/Shiny decision-support dashboard. It does not authenticate respondents, synchronise records between devices or host a secure shared database. Such functions require a separately secured backend.
